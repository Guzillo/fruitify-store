🍉 This is a demo project — just for fun!

Fruitify is a playground built for fun. Nothing here is real: even though you'll see a Stripe checkout, no payment is taken and nothing will ever be shipped. The goal is simply to help shopping addicts blow off some steam in a safe, consequence-free place.

Please don't enter any personal data — no real names, addresses, card numbers or anything else you wouldn't want to share publicly. Treat every form here as a toy.
